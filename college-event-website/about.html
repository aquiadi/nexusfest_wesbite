// Our Story

ABOUT THE FEST

Fifteen years of passion, culture, and unforgettable moments — all under one roof.

15 YEARS OF NEXUS FEST

NexusFest began in 2010 as a small inter-departmental quiz competition with fewer than 200 participants. Today it has grown into one of the most anticipated college festivals in the region, drawing students from over 60 colleges across the state.

The festival covers everything from hardcore tech competitions like hackathons and robotics, to cultural events like dance battles, open mics, and theatrical performances. We believe every student has a talent worth showcasing — NexusFest is that platform.

Organised entirely by student volunteers under the guidance of the Student Activities Council, the fest is a massive collaborative effort that starts planning six months before the actual event.

// Quick Answers

FAQ

Who can participate in NexusFest?

Any student currently enrolled in a recognized university or college can register. Some events have specific eligibility criteria — check the Events page for details.

Is registration free?

A nominal registration fee of ₹200 per person covers all three days. Certain premium workshops may have an additional fee. On-the-spot registrations are available but limited.

Are accommodations provided for outstation students?

Yes! We provide free hostel accommodation for registered outstation participants on a first-come, first-served basis. Mention this in the contact form while registering.

How do I get to the campus?

Greenwood University is located on NH-48, 12km from the city railway station. Shuttle buses will run every 30 minutes from the station during festival days.
